About Sri Lanka

Discover the Pearl of the Indian Ocean

Sri Lanka is a tropical island famous for its beautiful beaches, ancient heritage, lush mountains, wildlife, tea plantations and welcoming culture. In one journey you can climb a legendary rock fortress, watch leopards on safari, wander colonial forts, sip fresh Ceylon tea and end the day beside the Indian Ocean.

Compact, diverse and rich in tradition, Sri Lanka offers a rare blend of relaxation, culture, nature and adventure — all within a single unforgettable island.

Practical Guide

Essential Travel Tips

Plan confidently with these simple, useful tips for a smooth Sri Lankan journey.

Best time to visit

December to April is ideal for the west and south coasts, while May to September is best for the east coast and cultural triangle.

Getting around Sri Lanka

Use trains for scenic hill-country routes, private drivers for flexibility and tuk-tuks for short city trips.

Currency

The Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR) is the local currency. Cards are common in cities, but keep cash for markets and rural areas.

Weather

Sri Lanka is warm year-round. Pack light clothing, rain protection for hill country and modest attire for temples.

Local etiquette

Dress respectfully at religious sites, remove shoes before entering temples and ask before photographing people.

Safety tips

Sri Lanka is generally welcoming to travelers. Stay hydrated, use sunscreen, secure valuables and follow local guidance.
